01

Hash

Your GPU and CPU hash your address, a nonce, the last cat's work and a recent block. Your address is inside, so nobody can steal your answer.

02

Crack

A hash below the target is a ticket. The target retunes every 8 cats to keep about one cat every 30 seconds.

03

Pay

Send the ticket with the current price. 60% goes to living cats as rent, 30% buys and burns $CTASIC, 10% to the team.

04

Draw

The contract turns your hash into fur, pattern, ears, eyes, mouth, outfit, eyewear, headwear and the item in its paw.

hold

Your cat earns rent from every cat mined after it. Claim any time. Selling the cat sells its unclaimed rent too.

burn

Get its rent plus 1,000 $CTASIC in its own epoch, halving every epoch after. Its share of future rent goes to the cats still alive.

One cat per epoch is a one-of-one. GPU mining needs WebGPU (Chrome or Edge on a computer with a wallet extension); wallet in-app browsers mine with CPU. Mining costs ETH and gas. Rent depends on future mints and is never guaranteed.
